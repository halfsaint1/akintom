## About
Hero band (short) → Company story (img + text) → Mission/Vision/Values (3 cards) →
Leadership (profile cards) → Why students trust us (numbered list) →
UK partnerships (logo/name strip) → Achievements (stat band) → Final CTA.

## Services
Hero band → sticky in-page anchor nav → 6 alternating detail sections
(each: eyebrow, H3, copy, included-items checklist, CTA) → FAQ accordion → Final CTA.

## Testimonials
Hero band → filter pills (All / Undergraduate / Postgraduate / Scholarship / Visa) →
video testimonial cards (poster + play affordance) → written story grid (photo, ★, quote,
name, course, university) → placement strip → Final CTA.

## Events & News
Hero band → category pills (Study Abroad Tips / Scholarships / UK Universities /
Visa Updates / Events) → featured article → article grid (image, category badge,
date, title, excerpt) → newsletter strip → Final CTA.

## Contact
Hero band → 2-col: form (name, email, phone, preferred course, desired university,
message; validation + success state) | contact rail (WhatsApp button, phone, email,
office address, business hours) → Google Map embed → Final CTA.
