# Brand Snapshot — Akintom Global Resources

## Who they are
UK universities recruitment and admissions agency operating in Nigeria. They take Nigerian students (undergraduate, postgraduate, professional Master's) from first enquiry through admission, scholarship, visa, accommodation, and departure.

## Audience
- Nigerian students (17–35) seeking UK admission
- Parents and sponsors funding international education (key decision-makers; risk-averse, trust-driven)
- Working professionals targeting one-year UK Master's degrees
- Scholarship-dependent applicants
- UK partner universities (B2B audience: credibility of recruitment pipeline)

## Offer
Free consultation → guided placement into UK partner universities, with scholarship search, visa support, accommodation and pre-departure handled in one relationship.

## Differentiator (the ownable idea)
**"One named advisor, one accountable pathway."** Most Nigerian agents are volume processors: students are a file number, parents get no visibility, and the relationship ends at the offer letter. Akintom positions as a consultancy, not a processing desk — an offer-first strategy, parent-inclusive reporting, and support that continues until the student lands in the UK.

## Brand personality
Professional · Premium · Trusted · Global · Educational · Aspirational · Friendly

## Brand voice
- **Calm authority.** Short declarative sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- **Specific.** Numbers, named universities, named steps — never "world-class solutions".
- **Parent-aware.** Speaks to the sponsor's anxiety (money, safety, legitimacy) as much as the student's ambition.
- **Nigerian-to-UK fluency.** Understands WAEC/JAMB/HND-to-Master's conversion questions, proof-of-funds, CAS timelines.

## Trust architecture (in priority order)
1. Verifiable numbers (students placed, visa success rate, years operating)
2. Named partner universities with rankings
3. Real student stories with photo, name, course, university
4. Transparent 6-step process published openly
5. Free consultation (low-risk first step)
6. Physical office, phone, WhatsApp, business hours (we exist, come and see)

## Messaging hierarchy
1. **Promise:** Study in the UK with confidence.
2. **Mechanism:** Expert, end-to-end guidance — admission, scholarship, visa, arrival.
3. **Proof:** Placement numbers, visa success rate, partner universities, testimonials.
4. **Offer:** Free consultation. WhatsApp an advisor now.
5. **Reassurance:** Transparent process, parent reporting, no hidden fees.
