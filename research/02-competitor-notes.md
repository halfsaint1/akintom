# Competitive Field Notes — UK Study-Abroad Agencies (Nigeria)

## Category leaders reviewed
- **IDP Education** — corporate, process-driven, enormous trust signals (IELTS co-owner), but impersonal; students are funnel volume.
- **SI-UK Nigeria** — strong UK specialisation, event-led (fairs), heavy on logos, weak on individual student storytelling.
- **Study Group / Kaplan pathways** — institutional polish, speaks to universities more than to families.
- **Local Lagos/Abuja agents** — WhatsApp-first, fast, but visually untrustworthy: stock collages, no published process, no named team, "100% guaranteed visa" claims that sophisticated parents distrust.

## Repeated tropes to avoid
- Union Jack + Big Ben hero collages
- "Your gateway to global education" headline language
- Wall of country flags (dilutes the UK-only specialisation)
- Guarantee claims ("100% visa guaranteed") — non-credible and risky
- Carousel of 40 blurry university logos
- Generic blue + red colour schemes

## Gaps = opportunities
1. **Nobody publishes the actual process.** A transparent 6-step timeline is rare and is the single biggest trust unlock for parents.
2. **Nobody designs for the parent.** Sponsors fund the journey; almost no competitor addresses them directly.
3. **No premium visual register.** The category looks like budget travel agencies. Oxford-meets-Apple art direction is wide open.
4. **Weak proof.** Real names + courses + universities in testimonials immediately outclass the field.
5. **Local SEO is shallow.** "UK admission consultants in Nigeria" intent terms are winnable with structured content.

## Positioning sentence
*Akintom Global Resources is the premium UK-only admissions consultancy for Nigerian families — one named advisor guiding each student from first conversation to first lecture.*
